How Pay-As-You-Throw (PAYT) Works

Pay-As-You-Throw is a usage-based waste management system where users are charged directly based on the amount of waste they produce, rather than paying a flat rate.

1. Measure Waste

Your solid, dry, and e-waste is weighed at drop-off stations to compute precise environmental metrics.

2. Earn Incentives

Recyclables generate instant cash rebates or credits to offset regular municipal trash disposal fees.

3. Reduce Footprint

By making waste generation variable in cost, communities naturally divert recyclable goods away from landfills.

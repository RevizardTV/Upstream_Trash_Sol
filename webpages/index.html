Understanding the PAYT Mechanics

Pay-As-You-Throw penalizes unsorted disposal while rewarding source-separated recycling.

Earnings vs. Disposal Charges

  • Unsorted Trash (Fee Charged): Mixed municipal solid waste dumped for landfills incurs a per-kilogram fee billed directly to your profile.
  • Clean Recyclables (Cash Rebate): Segregated PET plastic, paper, metal, or e-waste earn direct cash credits based on market scrap rates.
  • Net Balance Settlement: Your recycling earnings offset municipal trash charges. Excess balances are released straight to your registered UPI address.

Regulatory Frameworks

  • Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules: Enforces mandatory doorstep segregation at source into wet, dry, and domestic hazardous waste streams.
  • Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR): Tracked weight logs generate verified recycling credits used to fulfill producer recycling quotas.
  • Audit Security: All scale readings and user transactions are immutable, digitally logged, and fully audit-compliant.

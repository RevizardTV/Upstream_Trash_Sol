Landfill Surcharges & PAYT Tipping Fees

Under the Pay-As-You-Throw (PAYT) municipal model, mixed non-recyclable solid waste intended for landfill disposal incurs a variable fee of ₹12.00 per kilogram. These tipping fees cover municipal hauling, environmental monitoring, and long-term carbon offsets. Surcharges are automatically debited from your EcoRecycle balance or billed to your monthly utility statement.

Contamination Penalties & Optical Audits

Smart Drop-off Stations employ optical AI and automated weight sensors to inspect incoming materials. Submitting non-recyclables inside recyclable bins or mixing organic waste with plastic/paper streams triggers an automatic ₹25.00 contamination penalty fee. Repeated infractions may result in account hold or reduced payout tiers.

Direct Financial Rewards & Credits

High-value recyclables are directly converted into financial credits. Clean sorted materials receive immediate payout rate credits, which can be withdrawn to registered bank accounts or used to offset landfill charges and municipal utility bills.

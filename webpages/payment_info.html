Pay-As-You-Throw (PAYT) Information

Understanding our weight-based recycling incentivization model and rate structure.

1

Separate & Tag

Segregate recyclables by material type into designated smart bin bags with QR codes.

2

Automated Weighing

Drop off at any station scale. Sensors deduct container tare weight automatically.

3

Instant Payouts

Receive payouts directly via UPI or Bank IMPS within 2 hours of verification.
